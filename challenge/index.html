Content guidelines

Unleash your glow.

Drop 3 original, high-quality videos featuring one (or all) of our products. You have total creative freedom — bring your authentic skincare journey to life.

Visual checklist · Mandatory

  • ✕
    No unboxing or boxes

    Skip the cardboard. Dive straight into the product and skin.

  • ✓
    Clear before vs. after

    Show that real skin transformation.

  • ✓
    Texture & application

    Zoom in on the rich formula and how it melts into the skin.

  • ✓
    Application shot required

    You don’t need to show your full face, but you must show the product being applied directly to the skin.

  • ✓
    High quality (HD)

    Low-resolution videos are difficult to evaluate. Please shoot in clear, high resolution.

  • ✓
    Stick to the guidelines

    Fully understand and include the core messages and mandatory requirements.
